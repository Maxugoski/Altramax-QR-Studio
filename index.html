Scanning Guidelines

  • Always test the QR code with your phone camera before printing it.
  • Ensure there is high contrast between the dots and the background.
  • If your logo is too large, the QR code might become un-scannable. Adjust the size to 20-30%.
  • Keep the background light for best readability with standard cameras.
